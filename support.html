Sleevo: TCG Card Scanner · effective October 9, 2026

Sleevo reads the small set number on a Pokémon or Yu-Gi-Oh! card — like 004/102, SV049/SV094 or LOB-EN001 — to find the exact printing and its price.

Common questions

  • It can't read the number. Tilt the card away from the light so the corner has no glare, or type the number in the field at the bottom of the scan screen.
  • Two cards have the same number. Some numbers exist in several sets. Sleevo shows the list — pick yours; the set mark (like SVI) narrows it.
  • Why is there no price? Some printings aren't priced by the market yet. They stay in your binder and are not counted in the total.
  • Restore my subscription. Open the paywall or Settings in Sleevo and tap Restore.
  • Cancel. Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions › Sleevo.
