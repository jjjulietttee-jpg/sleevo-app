Privacy Policy

Sleevo: TCG Card Scanner · effective October 9, 2026

Sleevo reads the set number printed on a trading card and looks up that exact printing and its market price. It is built to keep your collection on your iPhone. There is no account and no sign-in.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Camera frames. The camera image is read by Apple's on-device text recognition to find the card number. Frames are not uploaded and not recorded.
  • Card photos. When you add a scanned card, a cropped photo of your own card is saved inside the app on this device so you can recognise it in your binder. It is never uploaded.
  • Your binder. The cards you add, their condition and quantity, your daily binder totals and your setup answers (games, market, collection size) are stored only on this device. Deleting the app deletes them.

What leaves your iPhone

  • Card lookups. To identify a card and get its price, Sleevo sends the card's set number (for example 004/102 or LOB-EN001) from your device to the public card databases TCGdex (api.tcgdex.net) and YGOPRODeck (db.ygoprodeck.com). No photo, name, identifier or location is sent. These services see your IP address as with any web request; see their own policies.
  • Subscriptions. Purchases are processed by Apple. We use Adapty to check your subscription status and to understand, in aggregate, how the paywall performs (for example, which plan was chosen). Adapty receives an anonymous app-generated profile ID, device and purchase information — not your name or email. Adapty privacy policy: adapty.io/privacy.

What we don't do

No advertising SDKs, no tracking across apps, no selling of data, no analytics beyond the subscription data above. Sleevo is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

You can turn the camera off in Settings › Sleevo and type card numbers instead. You can remove any card from your binder, or delete the app to erase everything stored by it.
